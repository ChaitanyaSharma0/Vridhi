Career intelligence for the Indian workforce · Build for Bharat 2.0

What should I learn next, and what will it be worth?

SkillForge answers that one question with a four-stage pipeline. Job postings, salary records, resumes and university syllabi go in at the top. Skill gaps, salary forecasts and curriculum reports come out at the bottom, for students, recruiters and universities.

5 raw collections → NLP engine

The core of the system. One extraction function runs over job descriptions, resumes and syllabi alike and turns free text into a clean list of skills. Structured results go back to MongoDB; embeddings go to ChromaDB for semantic search.

Stage 1 · Extraction

Two passes. A dictionary match against ESCO catches the common skills fast. A Groq LLM pass picks up the emerging and niche ones the dictionary misses.

Details

extract, then normalise

Stage 2 · Normalisation

A curated set of 500+ skills from the EU's ESCO framework. Every spelling of a skill resolves to one canonical name, so "ML" and "machine learning" are counted together.

Details

Stage 3 · Vectors

MiniLM-L6-v2 turns skills and text chunks into 384-dimension vectors. Related skills land close together, which powers matching and RAG retrieval.

Details
processed records + vectors → intelligence modules

Four modules read the processed data and each answers a different question: what is trending, how far am I from a role, what am I worth, and does this course teach what employers want. Each one is trained on Indian data.

What's trending?

Counts skill mentions per month by role and city, flags skills as rising or declining past ±15%, and forecasts six months ahead with ARIMA and linear regression.

Details

How far am I?

Compares a resume against the ideal skill vector for the target role, built from every matching posting. Missing skills are ranked by match gain and salary impact.

Details

What am I worth?

An ensemble of Random Forest, XGBoost and gradient boosting predicts salary from skills, experience and city. A what-if simulator and SHAP values show what moves the number.

Details

Does this course keep up?

Scores each university course by its overlap with market demand and writes a PDF gap report a dean can act on.

Details

Supporting systems

500+ skills, 50+ roles and 15 sectors in NetworkX, rendered with pyvis. Edges weighted by demand.

Skill embeddings projected to 2D. Python sits near pandas, Docker near Kubernetes.

Every training run logged with hyperparameters, RMSE, R² and MAE. Versioned and reproducible.

Sample outputs

module outputs → counselor, dashboard, API

Where people meet the system. The counselor lets the LLM pick which module to call through function calling, so there is no hand-written routing. Streamlit is the dashboard, and FastAPI exposes every module to other platforms.

Talk to it

Groq reads the question, calls TalentMatch, CompIntel or SkillRadar as needed, pulls supporting context from ChromaDB, and answers only from what came back.
